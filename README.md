# VoltProfile

### Scan. Trust. Trade.

**AI-Powered Battery Intelligence and Trusted Trade-In Ecosystem**

VoltProfile is a proposed AI-powered battery intelligence and trusted trade-in ecosystem designed to bridge the information gap between EV servicing and resale. It brings together battery diagnostics, service records and vehicle-condition evidence into a unified, shareable digital trust profile to support transparent used-EV trade-ins.


## Overview

The used electric vehicle market faces a major challenge: the lack of accessible and trustworthy battery health information.

Battery diagnostics, service history and vehicle-condition reports are often fragmented across different stakeholders. This makes it difficult for buyers and dealers to evaluate battery condition, determine remaining battery life and justify trade-in or resale prices.

**VoltProfile addresses this challenge by combining AI-powered battery assessment with a digital trust card and vehicle-health-based trade-in insights.**

## The Problem

### The Missing Data Between Service and Sale

- **Limited Battery Health Transparency:** Buyers and dealers lack a clear, trusted view of battery health and remaining life.
- **Fragmented Vehicle Information:** Battery data, service history and vehicle-condition reports are not brought together in one place.
- **Unclear Parts History:** Unclear parts history and availability make repair and reuse decisions harder.
- **Uncertain Trade-In Valuations:** Without reliable health evidence, owners and dealers struggle to justify trade-in offers and resale prices.

The information flow involves multiple stakeholders:

`EV Owner → OEM / BMS Data → Service Centre → Dealer / Trade-In → Used-EV Buyer`

The gap in battery history between servicing and resale creates uncertainty throughout the used-EV transaction process.

## Our Solution

VoltProfile transforms fragmented battery diagnostics and service records into one trusted, evidence-backed passport for every used-EV trade-in.

### 1. AI Battery Assessment

Estimates battery health and remaining life, along with a confidence range.

### 2. VoltProfile Trust Card

Combines battery insights, service records and vehicle-condition evidence into one shareable digital profile.

### 3. Smarter Trade-In Decisions

Uses vehicle-health insights to recommend trade-in and resale price ranges.

### 4. Circular EV Ecosystem

Connects trusted vehicle information with repair, reuse and resale of parts.

## VoltLoop Architecture

The VoltLoop architecture represents the journey from EV health assessment to trusted resale.

| Component                          | Description                                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Owner App**                      | Collects vehicle photos, battery logs and service records.                       |
| **Collect & Verify**               | Collects and verifies vehicle evidence.                                          |
| **AI Health Assessment**           | Evaluates battery health, visible vehicle condition and fair value.              |
| **Virtual Battery Lab**            | Supports battery simulation, model training and validation.                      |
| **VoltProfile Digital Trust Card** | Combines battery health and vehicle history into a shareable digital profile.    |
| **Dealer Dashboard**               | Uses vehicle-health insights to support trade-in offers and resale price ranges. |
| **Buyer Marketplace**              | Enables EV search, comparison, verification and buying.                          |
| **Circular Parts**                 | Supports repair, reuse and resale of vehicle parts.                              |

### End-to-End Workflow

1. The EV owner submits vehicle photos, battery logs and service records.
2. Vehicle evidence is collected and verified.
3. The AI Health Assessment evaluates battery health, visible vehicle condition and fair value.
4. The Virtual Battery Lab supports simulation, training and validation of models.
5. Assessment results and vehicle history are combined into a VoltProfile Trust Card.
6. The owner can approve sharing of the digital trust card.
7. Dealers use the information to generate trade-in offers and resale price ranges.
8. Buyers can search, compare, verify and purchase used EVs.
9. Circular Parts supports repair, reuse and resale.

## Technology Stack

The proposed technology stack is organized into four layers.

| Layer                 | Technologies                                   | Purpose                                     |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Frontend**          | React, Tailwind CSS, Vite                      | Customer and dealer portals                 |
| **Backend**           | Node.js, Express, JWT, FastAPI                 | APIs, roles and authentication              |
| **AI Engine**         | Python, PyTorch, PyBaMM, OpenCV, LLM Assistant | Battery intelligence, simulation and vision |
| **Data & Deployment** | MongoDB Atlas, AWS, Docker, Vercel             | Storage, EV records and deployment          |

### AI Capabilities

The AI engine focuses on:

- **SoC (State of Charge):** Battery charge estimation.
- **SoH (State of Health):** Battery health assessment.
- **RUL (Remaining Useful Life):** Estimation of remaining battery life.
- **Vision:** Vehicle-condition assessment.

PyBaMM supports battery simulation, while PyTorch, OpenCV and the LLM Assistant form part of the proposed AI technology stack.
